The Problem and What It Solves

Ever had to rename your LODS, add prefixes or suffixes for baking or fix exports for Unreal Engine? Download the script from my repo, then simply select the meshes you want to rename, apply or remove the chosen prefix/suffix to the meshes. You can fully edit the text for prefix and suffix to fit your project needs and naming conventions. ​
